plak resume — open a prior session

USAGE:
  plak resume              Cwd-scoped session picker
  plak resume <id>         Open session in TUI (full uuid or the short id printed at quit)
  plak --resume [uuid]     Same as resume

  TUI: /resume lists cwd sessions; /resume <session> reopens an attached session only when its persisted locator, workspace, and binding match exactly.
