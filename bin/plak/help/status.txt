plak status — credential readiness

USAGE:
  plak status [codex|grok|claude|openai|xai-api|anthropic|openrouter]

  The SuperGrok subscription account answers to `grok` and `supergrok`.
  Bare `xai` is ambiguous and refused; use grok or xai-api.

  Warns when a compiled provider review expires within 14 days or is already expired.
