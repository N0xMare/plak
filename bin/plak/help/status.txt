plak status — credential readiness

USAGE:
  plak status [codex|grok|claude|openai|xai-api|anthropic|openrouter]

  The SuperGrok subscription account answers to `grok` and `supergrok`.
  Bare `xai` is ambiguous and refused; use grok or xai-api.
  All verticals also accept /<name>, such as claude/work or openai/work.

  Warns within 14 days of a subscription profile's expiry, or after it, and notes
  when documented API facts are within 14 days of their review-due date, or past it.
