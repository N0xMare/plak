plak logout — retire OAuth credentials

USAGE:
  plak logout <codex|grok|claude> [--local]

DESCRIPTION:
  Revokes a usable credential at the provider, then retires it locally.
  A credential this build cannot use (quarantined, login required, changed
  profile) is retired locally; its remote grant may remain active.
  With nothing stored, says there is nothing to log out.

OPTIONS:
  --local   Do not contact the provider; retire the local credential only

NOTES:
  Claude OAuth has no pinned remote revoke; logout retires the local credential.
