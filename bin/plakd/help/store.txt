plakd store — backup of the current SQLite stores

USAGE:
  plakd store backup <directory> [--stopped] [--budget-secs <seconds>]
  plakd store rehearse --into <directory>

Copy the runtime, product and agent-tree databases at a shared point in time.
Use a new directory outside PLAK_HOME under a parent you own. The private
backup directory contains the SQLite files and a SHA-256 manifest.
The live host can keep serving during the copy.

rehearse copies the product and agent-tree databases into <directory>/stores
and applies this build's pending revisions to the copies; the live stores are
only read. <directory> must be new and directly under the product home. Pass
<directory>/stores to session check-binding or check-reopen as --store-probe.

OPTIONS:
  --stopped                Require host and standalone writer vacancy during backup
  --into <directory>       The new rehearsal directory
  --budget-secs <seconds>  Maximum seconds to spend copying before timing out
                           (default: 600; override with PLAK_STORE_BACKUP_BUDGET_SECS)
