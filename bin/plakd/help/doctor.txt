plakd doctor — local health checks

USAGE:
  plakd doctor [--json] [--audit-runtime]

  --json prints the same checks and exit status as one JSON object.
  --audit-runtime scans and verifies the retained runtime store in a read-only snapshot.

  When an OpenRouter key and default model are set, probes catalog keys via discovery GETs only (no inference).

  Warns within 14 days of a subscription profile's expiry, or after it, and notes
  when documented API facts are within 14 days of their review-due date, or past it.
